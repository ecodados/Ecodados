01 / FROM EVIDENCE TO EXECUTION
01 / The context

The data already exists.

Across the world, climate initiatives produce valuable evidence on governance, public policies and climate finance.

EcoDados builds on existing climate information systems — including Painel ClimaBrasil and potentially Climate Scanner — to help decision-makers move from diagnosis to action. It complements, rather than replaces, the original sources.

Climate intelligence / Source-independent approach

Different sources.
One path to action.

The challenge is not to replace climate data platforms. It is to make their evidence more actionable.

EcoDados is conceived as a flexible intelligence layer for climate datasets from local, national and international initiatives. Each source may require its own data mapping and validation.

01 / ORIGINAL CONTEXTPainel ClimaBrasil

Brazilian climate governance, public policy and financing evidence — the starting point of EcoDados.

02 / INTERNATIONAL EXAMPLEClimate Scanner

A global climate assessment initiative illustrating the international potential of this approach. Integration subject to technical validation.

03 / EXTENSIBLE APPROACHOther climate data sources

Compatible public datasets and institutional assessments, mapped to their own structures and methodologies.

02 / The challenge

Information is powerful.
Direction changes things.

CHALLENGE / 01

The diagnosis is clear. The next step is not.

Even when the data reveals where a problem lies, an operational question remains: what should be prioritized, and what should happen next?

CHALLENGE / 02

Someone may have already solved this. But you may not know who.

Another city, region or country may have faced a similar challenge. Yet its experience is not always accessible to those making decisions today.

03 / The insight

What was missing was a bridge between data and execution.

04 / Introducing EcoDados

That bridge became EcoDados.

Designed to work across different climate data sources, EcoDados translates diagnosis into practical direction and connects decision-makers with experiences that can accelerate implementation.

10 / Our origin

Built during ClimatonBrasil 2026.

EcoDados was born during ClimatonBrasil 2026, using Painel ClimaBrasil as its original data context. Its vision extends beyond a single panel or country: connecting climate evidence from different sources to public action and shared learning.

An independent project developed during the hackathon. References to Painel ClimaBrasil and Climate Scanner identify relevant climate data initiatives, not institutional endorsement or a confirmed live integration.
